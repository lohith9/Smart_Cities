# Disclaimer

**The datasets in this repository are synthetic.**

They are statistically calibrated to published traffic, air-quality and meteorological priors
for Inner London. They do not contain real traffic counts, real air-quality measurements, or
any observational record. Live LAQN and TfL feeds were unreachable during the study; the
generator and its calibration targets are documented in Supplementary Appendix B and in
`code/01_generate_dataset.py`.

They exist solely to demonstrate the methodology presented in the chapter.

**No operational, planning, regulatory or public-health decision should be based on these
datasets or on results derived from them.** The reported scenario outcomes describe a designed
comparison under assumed inputs, not measured effects of any intervention.

The framework is offered as a research baseline that others can reproduce, validate against
live data, and extend. Validation against real measurements has not been performed.
